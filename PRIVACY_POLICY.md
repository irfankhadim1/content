# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Xiga eGFR.

## Information Collected

The app does not collect, transmit, or store personal information, health information, or calculator inputs and results. You can use the calculators without creating an account.

## On-Device Calculations

Calculator values are used on your device to calculate an estimate. The app does not save them for later use or send them to another service.

## Sharing and Retention

Because the app does not collect or retain your calculator data, it has no such data to share. Information remains only while you are using the relevant calculator screen and is not stored by the app.

## Contacting Us

If you choose to email us, your email address and message will be handled by your email provider and the recipient so they can respond. That information is provided by you outside the calculator and is not collected by the app.

For privacy questions, contact: [irfanali.54@hotmail.com](mailto:irfanali.54@hotmail.com).

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or its practices change. The latest version will be available in the app.
